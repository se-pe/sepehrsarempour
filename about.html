Profile

Research comes first.

I look at the audience, the market and what others have done. I look for different angles before deciding whether a project needs a familiar approach or could benefit from going in a different direction.

Exploration

I like a challenge, especially when there’s room to make something outside the usual expectations. An unconventional direction still has to answer the brief. I explore it, explain what it offers and work through it with the client.

Working together Commercial and cultural

Explore, discuss,
refine.

I explore different directions, explain the decisions behind them and refine the work through feedback. Research gives us a starting point; discussing the work helps us decide what to take further.

AI in my process

I use AI as an assistant for research, gathering information and coding. I also create AI-generated images and videos when they suit a project, then edit and refine the results before presenting them. I discuss that use with the client before we begin. The design decisions and final judgment stay with me.
